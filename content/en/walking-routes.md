# Walking routes

Walking routes are drawn on the 3D map. Each one shows its **distance**, **total climb** and an **estimated walking time**, based on about 4.5 km/h plus 1 minute for every 10 m of climb, because HKU is steep.

## View routes

Open the [3D map](map) and tap the **routes** button. Tap a route to draw it on the terrain and fly to it.

> [!NOTE]
> No routes have been published yet. Routes are drawn by contributors who walk them, so they match real paths, stairs and lifts. Want to add one, such as "HKU Station → Main Library" or "Centennial → Knowles"? See below.

## Draw a route

1. Open the map and turn on **✎ Edit**.
2. Tap **Draw route**, then tap points along the path in order: start, each turn, each lift, end. Points snap to the ground or rooftops you tap.
3. Tap **Copy route JSON** and paste it into `data/routes.json` on GitHub. See [Contribute & edit](contribute).
