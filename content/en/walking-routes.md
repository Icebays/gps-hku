# Walking routes

Walking routes are drawn on the 3D map. Each one shows its **distance**, **total climb** and an **estimated walking time**, based on about 4.5 km/h plus 1 minute for every 10 m of climb, because HKU is steep.

## View routes

Open the [3D map](map) and tap the **routes** button. Tap a route to draw it on the terrain and fly to it.

> [!NOTE]
> No routes have been published yet. Routes are added by the GPS-HKU maintainers after walking them, so they match real paths, stairs and lifts.
