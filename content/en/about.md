# About & credits

**GPS-HKU** is a 3D map, walking guide and floor plan guide to the University of Hong Kong, made by students. It was inspired by the [Survive in HKU Manual](https://ric-hku.gitbook.io/survive-hku-manual).

## Data

- **3D buildings, infrastructure and terrain with aerial imagery**: 3D Spatial Data, © Lands Department, The Government of the Hong Kong SAR. Tiles 11-SW-7C and 11-SW-7D were optimised for the web: merged, simplified, textures resized and the geometry compressed.
- **Building names and map numbers**: from the public HKU campus map.
- **Lift and ramp information**: summarised from the HKU Estates Office's wheelchair-friendly routes page.

## Built with

[three.js](https://threejs.org) for 3D, [marked](https://marked.js.org) for Markdown and [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev) with meshoptimizer for model compression. It's hosted for free on GitHub Pages.

## Disclaimer

GPS-HKU is not affiliated with or endorsed by The University of Hong Kong. Building data may be out of date, especially where construction is ongoing. Follow on-site signs and official HKU information, and in an emergency call **999**.
