# How to use GPS-HKU

GPS-HKU is laid out like a handbook. Chapters are in the left sidebar (tap ☰ on a phone), and the search box at the top finds both pages and buildings.

## Find a building

1. Type a name into the top search bar, or the search box on the map, for example `Main Building` or `本部`.
2. If the building is labelled on the map, you'll fly straight to it and it will light up in gold.
3. If it isn't labelled yet, you'll go to its entry in the [Building directory](buildings).

## Read the 3D map

- **Gold building**: the place you selected.
- **Solid white buildings**: HKU buildings. Click one for details.
- **See-through buildings**: non-HKU buildings, shown for context only. They don't respond to clicks.
- **Dashed outlines**: the Main Campus and Centennial Campus.
- **Map colours**: green is vegetation, light green is gardens, white is roads, orange is main roads, red lines are outdoor footpaths and trails, grey dots are passages inside buildings, blue is water, pale is built-up ground, with a contour line every 10 m. To see the aerial photo, tick **Aerial photo** in the layers panel (the hills behind campus stay as contours).
- **⚠︎ Approximate position**: some places are not yet confirmed.
- **Pins**: labelled places. Use the layers button to show or hide categories.
- **Heights**: shown in metres above Principal Datum (**mPD**), the standard height reference in Hong Kong. Compare two buildings' mPD values to see which one sits higher up the hill.

## Language

Use the **繁 / EN** button at the top right to switch between Traditional Chinese and English. Your choice is remembered, and links you share keep their language.

## On your phone

GPS-HKU works in any modern mobile browser. On the map, tap 🎯 to show your GPS position. Your browser will ask for permission first, and your location never leaves your phone.
