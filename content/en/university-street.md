# Upper & Lower University Street

The most confusing thing about HKU for newcomers is **University Street**, the indoor and outdoor spine that joins Centennial Campus to the old main campus. It has **two levels**:

| Level | Same as | Where you can reach it |
|---|---|---|
| **Upper University Street** | 5/F Haking Wong Building | G/F The Jockey Club Tower · the footbridge from MTR Exit A1 · 2/F near the Main Library entrance |
| **Lower University Street** | 4/F Haking Wong Building | LG/F The Jockey Club Tower (towards Lee Shau Kee Lecture Centre) · 1/F Hui Oi Chow corridor |

## Why the floor numbers don't match

Each building numbers its floors from its own ground entrance, and every building sits at a different height on the hill. So **"5/F" in one building can be the same level as "G/F" in the next**. When directions don't make sense, think in **levels** (Upper/Lower University Street, Sun Yat-sen Place), not floor numbers.
