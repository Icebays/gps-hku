# Welcome to GPS-HKU

<p class="lead">Lost between Upper and Lower University Street? Can't find the lift to Main Building? GPS-HKU is a student-made 3D map, walking guide and floor plan guide to the University of Hong Kong.</p>

HKU is built on a hillside. A building's "G/F" can sit at a completely different height from the one next door, and buildings are joined by bridges, podiums and lift towers. A flat map doesn't show that well, so GPS-HKU uses a real 3D model of the campus, built from the Lands Department's 3D spatial data.

```cards
🧭 | Open the 3D map | Turn, zoom and tap any building on campus. | map
🏛 | Building directory | Every building, with its map number and floor plans. | buildings
🛗 | Lifts & step-free routes | Which lift goes to which level. | lifts-and-ramps
🚇 | Getting to HKU | From the MTR to your first lecture. | getting-to-hku
```

## What's inside

- **3D campus map**: a model of the main campus and Centennial Campus. Tap a building to see its name, height and guide, or use GPS to see where you are.
- **Walking routes**: routes drawn on the 3D terrain, with distance, climb and estimated walking time.
- **Floor plans**: pan-and-zoom floor plans for each building, floor by floor.
- **Guides**: how University Street works, where the lifts go, and how to get here.

## This is a living guide

GPS-HKU is built by students, for students. Some buildings are **not labelled on the map yet**; more are added as the guide grows.

> [!NOTE]
> GPS-HKU is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with The University of Hong Kong. For official information, check the HKU and Estates Office websites.
