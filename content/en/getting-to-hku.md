# Getting to HKU

## By MTR

**HKU Station** on the **Island Line** is the closest station. It sits deep underground, so use the **lift towers** rather than climbing the stairs.

| Exit | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| **A1 / A2** | A lift tower to Pokfulam Road at street level. From A1, a footbridge-level stop leads to **Upper University Street**, which is 5/F of Haking Wong Building. |
| **C1** | A lift tower up to the **Central Podium Level of Centennial Campus**. |

> [!TIP]
> Going to Centennial Campus? Use **C1**. Going to the old main campus (Main Library, Haking Wong, Knowles)? Use **A1** and the footbridge to University Street.

## By bus or minibus

Many bus and green minibus routes stop along **Pokfulam Road** and **Bonham Road**, which run past the campus. Check the KMB/Citybus apps or the HK eTransport app for the latest routes.

## On the 3D map

Open the [3D map](map) and tap 🎯 to see where you are relative to the campus. Exits will appear as pins once they are labelled.
