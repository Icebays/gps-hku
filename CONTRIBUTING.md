# Contributing 參與貢獻

**English** · [繁體中文](#繁體中文)

## English

GPS-HKU is maintained by its owner and is **not open source** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)): the site is edited by the
maintainer only. You can still help a lot by telling us what is wrong or missing.

### How you can help

| You found… | Where to tell us |
|---|---|
| A building, place, pin or outline in the wrong place, or with the wrong name | [Discussions → General](https://github.com/icebays/gps-hku/discussions) |
| An eatery's details out of date (hours, ordering link, photos) | Discussions |
| A floor plan that does not match the building | Discussions |
| Something that does not work (a bug) | an [issue](https://github.com/icebays/gps-hku/issues) |
| An idea for a new feature | Discussions → Ideas |
| A security problem | **not in public**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

### A good report

- **Where**: a link to the place on the map (copy the page address, e.g. `#/en/map?place=main-library&floor=2`) or the
  building's name.
- **What**: what is shown, and what it should be.
- **How you know**: a photo you took, an official HKU page, a sign on site.
- For a bug: your phone or computer, your browser, and the steps that lead to it; a screenshot helps.

### Pull requests

Pull requests are not accepted unless agreed with the maintainer first. Please start with a Discussion or an issue.

### Your suggestions

By sending a correction, suggestion or other material, you agree that the maintainer may use it in GPS-HKU, without
any obligation to you. Do not send material you do not have the right to share, or other people's personal
information.

Please follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## 繁體中文

GPS-HKU 由擁有者維護，**並非開源項目**（見 [LICENSE](LICENSE)），網站只由維護者編輯。不過你仍然可以告訴我們哪裏錯了或欠缺甚麼，這已經是很大的幫助。

### 你可以怎樣幫忙

| 你發現… | 請在這裏告訴我們 |
|---|---|
| 建築、地點、標記或範圍的位置或名稱錯誤 | [Discussions → General](https://github.com/icebays/gps-hku/discussions) |
| 餐廳資料過時（營業時間、點餐連結、相片） | Discussions |
| 樓層平面圖與實際不符 | Discussions |
| 某功能無法正常運作（錯誤） | 開一個 [issue](https://github.com/icebays/gps-hku/issues) |
| 新功能建議 | Discussions → Ideas |
| 安全問題 | **請勿公開**：見 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

### 一份好的回報

- **在哪裏**：地圖上該地點的連結（複製網址，例如 `#/zh/map?place=main-library&floor=2`）或建築名稱。
- **錯在哪裏**：現在顯示甚麼、應該是甚麼。
- **你如何得知**：你拍的相片、港大官方網頁、現場指示牌。
- 如屬錯誤：你的手機或電腦、瀏覽器，以及重現的步驟；附截圖更好。

### Pull request

除非事先與維護者協定，否則不接受 pull request。請先開 Discussion 或 issue。

### 你的建議

當你提交更正、建議或其他材料，即表示你同意維護者可於 GPS-HKU 中使用，而毋須對你負任何責任。請勿提交你無權分享的材料或他人的個人資料。

請遵守[行為守則](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)。
